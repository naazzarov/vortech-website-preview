SMS Filtering & Anti-Fake SMS

AI-based filtering engine that blocks spam, fraud, and grey-route SMS in real time, with advanced protection against fraudulent or spoofed SMS by verifying sender authenticity.

  • Reduces customer churn by eliminating fraudulent messaging.
  • Protects network resources from malicious traffic spikes.
  • Real-time analysis of messaging patterns and sender verification.
