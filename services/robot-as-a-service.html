Overview

Robot as a Service (RaaS) makes our robotics line accessible on a subscription or deployment model instead of a capital purchase. Clients get the hardware, the AI software that drives it, fleet monitoring, and maintenance in one contract — from hospitality service robots to industrial mechanical arms.
